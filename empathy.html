Empathy Scholar

Empathy is the ultimate technology

Vince argues that empathy, the ability to deeply understand another person's experience, is the most important capability for founders, leaders and the AI systems of the future.

Core ideas

The Age of Empathy

🌍

The 21st century is the Age of Empathy

Industrial-age companies were built on efficiency, and information-age companies on data. Vince argues that the next generation of enduring companies will be built on understanding people.

🤝

Empathy-driven entrepreneurship

The best founders fall in love with the problem and the people who live it, not with their solution. Empathy is how they find problems worth solving.

🎨

Empathy in design

In his talk AI/UI: A Harmony Built for One Another, Vince explores how building empathy into design helps AI and user interfaces work together.

🤖

Empathic & explainable AI

As AI becomes part of every product, Vince advocates for systems that are transparent and explainable, and that are designed around human needs and dignity.

🧠

Empathy as a learnable skill

Empathy is not a fixed personality trait. People can practise it, measure it and strengthen it, as individuals, teams and organisations.

🌱

Empathy for impact

Empathy turns technology into a force for good. It helps innovators serve underserved communities and build an open, equal digital future.

♡
Initiative

Future of Empathy

Vince founded Future of Empathy, which he proposed as a public benefit initiative. Its goal is to explain why empathy matters, and to give people practical, open-source tools to express it.

The initiative connects research, entrepreneurship and technology, and asks one question: how can the tools we build make us more human, not less?

Framework

Empathy in practice: for founders

1️⃣

Listen

Spend time with the people who have the problem before you write a line of code.

2️⃣

Feel

Understand the emotions behind the behaviour: frustration, fear, hope.

3️⃣

Reframe

Turn those insights into a sharper problem statement.

4️⃣

Build & learn

Prototype, gather feedback, reinvent, and keep listening.

This framework is a summary of design-thinking principles that Vince teaches as a mentor. Ask Vince to review the exact wording before publishing.
