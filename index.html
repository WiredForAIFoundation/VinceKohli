What he does

Where empathy meets technology

Vince's work spans four connected areas. Each one aims at the same goal: helping people build companies that matter.

💜

Empathy Scholar

He researches and advocates for empathy as a core capability for leaders, products and AI systems. He also founded the Future of Empathy initiative.

🚀

Impact Tech Investor

As an early-stage venture and angel investor, he backs mission-driven founders in AI, Web3, deep tech and enterprise software.

🧭

Mentor & Startup Judge

He has judged and mentored startups at MIT, Stanford, UC Berkeley, Google, Samsung, IBM, NASA and other leading accelerators.

Speaker & Author
