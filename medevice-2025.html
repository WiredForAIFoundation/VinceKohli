About the event

MEDevice Silicon Valley is an MD&M event that brings together medical device engineers, innovators and suppliers. The 2025 show took place on November 19–20, 2025 at the Santa Clara Convention Center.

The Startup Stadium gives early-stage companies first-hand experience of pitching new technologies to the medtech community. On the Trends & Technology stage, Vince moderated the competition and judged as an investor. He gave founders feedback on their problem, product, business model and go-to-market strategy, then presented the Certificates of Excellence to the standout teams.

For Vince, events like this are empathy in action: helping founders understand the patients, clinicians and investors they need to win over.
