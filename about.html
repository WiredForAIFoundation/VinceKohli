About

From enterprise architect to empathy scholar

Vince has spent nearly two decades in Silicon Valley technology, building, advising, investing and teaching. One idea ties it all together: empathy is the ultimate innovation skill.

Vince Kohli is a Silicon Valley early-stage venture capital investor, seven-time tech entrepreneur, serial business builder, product visionary, inventor, "problem-thinking doer," public speaker and author.

He began his career in enterprise technology consulting at Accenture. He spent more than 16 years there, starting as an execution architect, with deep expertise in SAP ERP and large-scale business transformation. That experience taught him how large organisations really work, and why so many of them struggle to innovate.

He went on to found and build several technology ventures, and became a super angel investor, product executive and corporate strategist. He applies innovation-driven entrepreneurship and design thinking to help startups and global corporations find their next stage of growth.

He is the founder of Trillion Global Capital and Editor-in-Chief of Wired For AI, where he covers generative AI, agentic AI, AI leadership and emerging cybersecurity startups. He has also served as an Entrepreneur-in-Residence at IE Business School's Venture Lab and at Plug and Play.

Today he describes himself as an Empathy Scholar. He believes the defining skill of the 21st century, for founders, leaders and the AI systems we build, is the ability to deeply understand other people.
